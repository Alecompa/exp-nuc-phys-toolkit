Scope: reaction entry, EXFOR exploration, per-reaction astrophysics inputs, in-beam γ energies and contaminants,
particle (elastic) spectra, and a new measured-spectrum analysis tool. Three independent read-only reviews

confirmed findings were fixed with regression tests.

## New

- EXFOR: S factor at c.m. energies by default, tap-to-identify points, pinch zoom with y auto-fit, Gamow-window band,
  S(E) model overlay, inverse-kinematics lookup.
- Recent and saved reactions; parser accepts ¹²C, α/alpha/proton, (p,p′); single capitals (H, N, P, A) are no longer
  read as ³He, n, p, α. Beam-energy stepper in the reaction header of the run-planning tools.
- Contaminant and room-background library for the γ spectrum (ENSDF energies and lifetimes retrieved 2026-11-20,
  capture primaries from AME2020); each source lists one complete cascade, other branchings are left out.
- Spectrum Analysis: import (.Spe, .Chn, text/CSV), peak search (Mariscotti filter, SNIP), automatic calibration by
  pair voting and weighted least squares, peak identification including escape peaks.

## Limits

- In-flight emission uses the full recoil velocity (no slowing-down/DSAM shape) and one exponential with the mean
  decay time for fed levels.
- One Compton/peak ratio for all lines; no pair continuum, summing or pile-up.
- Peak areas are moment sums above SNIP, not fits; good for orientation, not final yields.
- Particle mode still ignores target depth, backing plateaus and excited ejectile groups.
- No iOS 26 simulator on the development Mac: UI verified by compiling for iOS and rendering charts off-screen on
  macOS; gestures (pinch, tap readout) still need a device check.
